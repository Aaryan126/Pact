Common Checks

  • Make sure camera access is enabled in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera.
  • Restart Clarity after changing camera permissions.
  • Keep your Mac awake during active focus sessions.

Privacy Summary

Clarity processes camera frames locally on your Mac. It does not store video, upload images, request microphone access, or require an account.
