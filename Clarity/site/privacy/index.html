Clarity Privacy Policy

Clarity is a local-first macOS app for measuring focus during work and study sessions.

Camera Processing

Clarity uses your Mac camera during active sessions to classify focus states locally on your device. Camera frames are processed in memory and discarded. Clarity does not record, store, upload, or transmit video, images, screenshots, or audio.

No Microphone Access

Clarity does not request microphone access.

No Accounts Or Analytics

Clarity does not require an account and does not collect analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or usage data.

Local Session Data

The app stores session records locally on your Mac, including session timestamps, detected focus states, durations, and user corrections. This data remains on your device unless you choose to export it.

Exports

Exports are created only when you explicitly choose an export destination.

Third Parties

Clarity does not sell data and does not share personal data with third parties.
